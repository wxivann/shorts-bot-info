# Privacy Policy – Shorts Bot

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Shorts Bot is a personal automation tool used by its owner to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own YouTube channel.

## What data the app accesses
- Through Google OAuth, the app only requests permission to **upload videos** (`youtube.upload`) to the YouTube channel of the person who authorizes it (the owner).

## How data is used
- The authorization token is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos.
- The app does not read, collect, sell, share or store personal data of any other user.

## Storage and sharing
- The token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository.
- No data is shared with third parties.

## Revoking access
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Contact
- For questions, contact the owner through the email listed on this app's Google Cloud project.
